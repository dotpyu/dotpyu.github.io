Peilin Yu

Apple Inc.

I’m a research engineer at Apple, where I conduct research on advancing multimodal intelligence technologies. My work focuses on building scalable systems that safely integrate language, vision, and other modalities in support of accessible, practical and user-facing AI experiences.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Brown University in 2025, advised by Prof. Stephen Bach. My research explores how to improve data strategy and annotation efficiency with weak supervision using foundation models, particularly in settings with noisy, limited or indirect supervision.

Previously, I interned at Microsoft Azure AI, where I worked on adapting multimodal large language models to improve performance across downstream tasks.

I’m also a proud 👐🏟🧀 Badger, having earned my B.S. in Computer Sciences and Mathematics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison in 2019.

Aviation is a long-standing passion of mine: I’m also an FAA-certified Private Pilot and Advanced Ground Instructor (AGI)—not the kind of AGI everyone’s hyped up about 🙂 (though I for sure like that kind too), but the kind focused on teaching humans the knowledge required to fly safely and confidently.
